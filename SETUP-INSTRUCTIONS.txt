SUN COAST AIR BNB FIJI — SETUP

Package files:
- index.html: refreshed responsive guest feedback form and CSS
- suncoast-logo.png: supplied logo image asset
- google_apps_script_email_pdf.gs: Google Apps Script PDF email service

Apps Script setup:
1. Visit https://script.google.com and create a new project.
2. Paste google_apps_script_email_pdf.gs into Code.gs and save.
3. Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
4. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.
5. Deploy and authorize permissions; copy the Web app URL.
6. In index.html, replace PASTE_YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE with that URL.
7. Keep index.html and suncoast-logo.png together, then publish them on your web host.
8. Submit a test and verify PDF delivery to iliutaki@gmail.com and mliutaki@gmail.com.

The form does not use FormSubmit. Submission is not active until the deployment URL is added and tested. Browser no-cors mode cannot confirm delivery, so verify both inboxes and spam folders.
